# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Agent Pantry a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and evidence rather than people.
- Accept correction and make room for contributors who are learning.
- Keep discussions focused on improving the project and its community.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, trolling, and sustained disruption are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and comments that violate this code. Repeated or severe violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method on their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential to the extent practical.
